# Privacy Policy

ATG Traders respects your privacy and protects the personal information needed to operate your account and provide the service.

## Information we collect

We may collect the following information when you create an account or use the platform:

- full name
- country of residence
- nationality
- phone number
- date of birth
- optional residential address
- account security question and answer
- password hash and authentication metadata
- profile activity, sign-in history, and security logs
- course progress, wallet activity, payment proof submissions, and message history needed for platform operation
- push notification registration data for native devices, if enabled

## How we use the information

We use your information to:

- create and secure your account
- verify your identity for access control and membership
- provide educational content, signals, course progress, and community features
- manage wallet, subscription, and purchase flows
- support admin review, dispute handling, and account safety
- deliver security notifications and platform service updates

## Data protection

- Passwords and security answers are not stored in plain text.
- Sensitive values are hashed or stored only where required for account security.
- Access to account data is restricted to authorized platform processes and administrators.
- Payment and wallet records are stored for operational, compliance, and audit purposes.

## Sharing of information

We do not sell personal data. We may share information only when:

- required to operate the platform and deliver services
- necessary for fraud prevention, account security, or legal compliance
- required by a valid legal process or regulatory request

We do not share your private security answer with third parties.

## Account deletion and data access

You may request access to personal data or deletion of your account by contacting the support email designated for the live app. Deletion requests may be subject to legal retention obligations for financial or audit records.

## Cookies and device data

The web app may use standard session cookies or local browser storage for sign-in continuity and app experience. Native mobile clients may store minimal, encrypted session data required for authentication.

## Push notifications

If push notifications are enabled for native devices, we store token information needed to deliver notifications to the correct device. You may disable notifications in the app settings or device settings.

## Third-party services

The platform may rely on third-party infrastructure for hosting, payment processing, notification delivery, and analytics. Those services are used only as required to operate the app.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the app or service notice.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the ATG support address used in the public store listing and app settings.
